# EW Frontend Design Specifications

This folder holds the design specification for the Enterprise Wallet (EW) frontend
(`ew-frontend`), the UI counterpart of the Enterprise Wallet backend and of the
LWA design specifications.

The Enterprise Wallet frontend is the web application used by Marketing Authorisation
Holders and National Competent Authorities to publish, maintain and audit medicinal
product data (products, batches and electronic product information documents) on the
PharmaLedger Product Traceability Platform (PTP).
